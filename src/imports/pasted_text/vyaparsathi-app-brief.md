Build a complete, modern, responsive web application prototype called “VyaparSathi”.

PROJECT PURPOSE:
VyaparSathi is an AI-powered, hyper-local business advisory platform designed for aspiring entrepreneurs and existing small business owners in India, especially rural and semi-urban users.

The platform should guide a user through the complete business journey:

Right Opportunity → Right Plan → Right Funding → Right Guidance → Sustainable Growth

The UI must be simple, modern, trustworthy, easy to understand, and accessible for users with low digital literacy.
